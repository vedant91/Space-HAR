1. Runtime: what runs on board

2. How HMR fits

  • What it adds: a 3D body measured against the rack. That gives us the astronaut's attitude (roll, pitch, yaw relative to the rack) and 3D wrist positions when 2D keypoints are unreliable. It also drives the 3D panel in the dashboard.
  • When it's used: the fusion gate decides every frame. If ROMP is confident and the skeleton passes a bone-length sanity check, we use its 3D wrists and attitude. Otherwise we use MediaPipe 2D.
  • When it fails today: on our renders ROMP loses the astronaut at ≥30° roll, 60° pitch and upside down. The 2D branch with roll correction takes over, and the step decision keeps working because it's driven mostly by boxes and hands.
  • Training consequence: the LSTM must be trained on the same fused features it will see live, both with and without HMR. Otherwise switching HMR on changes its input and accuracy silently drops. The builder records this as its own pose source (fused), and the existing load-time guard enforces it.

Why it's slow and wrong at the same time, and the fix

  • Cheap steps run every frame (YOLO person box, MediaPipe shoulder–hip vector) — both already computed for other reasons, so this costs nothing new.
  • ROMP only ever sees a small, upright, corrected crop, never the raw 1080p frame. This is the single highest-leverage change: it cuts the pixels ROMP's backbone processes by roughly an order of magnitude, and gives it the upright, well-resolved body it was actually trained to expect.
  • The scheduler decides when to re-run ROMP at all, based on how fast the cheap rotation estimate is changing — not a fixed "every Nth frame". An astronaut holding still while examining a box (most of a step) costs nothing; a fast tumble triggers frequent re-checks.
  • Prove it on the same 8 takes that fail today. HMR_UNRELIABLE_TAGS in tools/build_sequences.py is the exact before/after test: measure ROMP's detection rate on those 8 takes with and without this pipeline, rather than assuming it helps.

3. Training: how the models are made

The sequence builder runs the trained detector rather than the Blender ground-truth boxes, so the LSTM learns from the same imperfect detections it will see live.
